Transients include a large amount of objects across the entire electromagnetic spectrum, it is generally used an umbrella term to include any astronomical objects that is time-dependent on times scales from nanoseconds to years. This thesis focuses on transients that emit radio waves $(\lambda \approx \text{mm}-\text{km})$. \\ 
Radio waves can be emitted from an astrophysical object by either thermal or non-thermal emission. Any particle above 0~K vibrates when charged particles in atoms undergo acceleration they release electromagnetic radiation. As a consequence of this any particle above 0~K emits thermal radiation through two mechanisms blackbody or bremsstrahlung emission.
