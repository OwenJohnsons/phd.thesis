\section{Technosignature Searches with NenuFAR from 30-85~MHz}

The LT13 program builds upon the ES13 program. Both programs are dedicated to high frequency
resolution follow-up observations of exoplanet candidates detected with the Transiting Exoplanet Survey
Satellite (TESS). The purpose of these observations is to search for low-frequency technosignatures from
the exoplanet candidates, and to expand the multi-frequency / multi-telescope TESS observing program
led by Breakthrough Listen at the University of California at Berkeley. [1]
The nature of the program itself, aiming at detecting evidence of communicative extraterrestrial
civilizations, equally caters for non-exoplanet-targeted observations. As such, the team made a significant
contribution into the development of a commensal acquisition pipeline allowing the collection of raw
beam-formed data from any collaborating observer to be collected by the SETI back-end SETIBK.

\subsection{Target selection and sensitivity analysis}

274 observations of confirmed GAIA exoplanets have been conducted over the span of Early Science period and the first semester of the Science period. The distribution of pointings in the Galactic plane is illustrated in Figure 1. 

A conic search of the Gaia catalogue was conducted for each pointing of the surveyed targets within the Full Width at Half Maximum (FWHM) of the bore sight pointing. This resulted in a substantial number of targets, totalling over 3.5 million. Consistent with [2], the Gaia targets underwent filtering based on their coordinate values and distance values. These filters can be described as follows: firstly, stars with errors extending beyond the area enclosed by the FWHM were excluded.
